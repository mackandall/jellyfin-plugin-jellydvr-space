Convert to HEVC

Movies captured as heavy H.264 (1080p, before the Arc card) are re-encoded on the Arc as HEVC, one at a time. Each one is first checked for gaps: a recording that lost signal is better re-recorded than converted. The new copy is written beside the movie, checked (same length, same audio tracks), and takes the movie's place under its own name; the original is kept hidden for Undo.
