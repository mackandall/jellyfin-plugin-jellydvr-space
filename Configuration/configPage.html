3

Storage folders

Every place recordings can live outside the recording folders, each with its own floor: nothing is moved in if it would leave less free than that. Folders ticked "Use when recording drive is low" are used by the space manager, in this order, for the kinds they take; the shows below pick their folders from this list. Each folder must be inside a library, or moved recordings would disappear from Jellyfin. Recordings keep their show / season folders below it.
Separated by commas. Sports recordings go to "Sports" folders first, then to TV or Movies ones. The item menu's "Move to archive…" offers the folders for the recording's kind.
4

Where shows are stored

Checked with the space check: every few minutes and after each recording.
A finished recording of the show goes to the first of its folders that stays above that folder's floor; with none having room it stays where it is. Checked before the category rules.
5

Category rules

Jellyfin files movies and series in their own folders; everything else (comedy specials, one-off games, …) lands in the general recording folder. These rules move those by their guide categories, tried in order: the first one a recording matches decides where it goes. Tick "series too" to also take matching recordings out of the series folder (games come with a series id). Empty destination = the movie recording folder.
