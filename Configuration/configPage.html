3

Storage folders

Every place recordings can live outside the recording folders, each with its own floor: nothing is moved in if it would leave less free than that. Folders ticked "Use when recording drive is low" are used by the space manager, in this order, for the kinds they take; the shows below pick their folders from this list. Each folder must be inside a library, or moved recordings would disappear from Jellyfin. Recordings keep their show / season folders below it. "+ Add folder" picks one from the drives the server sees; "Add to library…" puts a folder in a library.
New folders start with: keep free % of their drive · ·
Separated by commas. Sports recordings go to "Sports" folders first, then to TV or Movies ones. The item menu's "Move to archive…" offers the folders for the recording's kind.

Where shows are stored

Checked with the space check: every few minutes and after each recording.
A finished recording of the show goes to the first of its folders that stays above that folder's floor; with none having room it stays where it is. Checked before the category rules.
5

Category rules

Jellyfin files movies and series in their own folders; everything else (comedy specials, one-off games, …) lands in the general recording folder. These rules move those by their guide categories, tried in order: the first one a recording matches decides where it goes. Tick "series too" to also take matching recordings out of the series folder (games come with a series id). Empty destination = the movie recording folder.

Archive automatically

Checked with the space check: every few minutes and after each recording.
After that it goes anyway (channels Comskip skips never get a scan). A failed scan counts as done. 0 = don't wait.
An incomplete recording Episode History is going to record again stays put so the new copy replaces it. 0 = don't wait.
One at a time; the rest wait for the next check, so a long first backlog doesn't hold everything up.
Moves finished recordings from the recording folders to the storage folders (section 3) once they are processed: finished, joined if split, scanned for commercials, not waiting to be recorded again, and not being played. Rules are tried in order; the first one a recording matches decides. No folders chosen = any storage folder that takes the recording's kind, in the list's order, above its floor. Never moved: recordings marked Keep, live TV buffers, shows with their own folders (section 4) and recordings with their own storage choice.

JDVR devices

What each TV running JDVR may do. These only narrow what its signed-in user may do in Jellyfin (Dashboard › Users); they never add to it. JDVR hides what a TV may not do, and the server refuses it. Other apps aren't affected. A TV picks up a change within a few minutes. Remove takes a TV off the list and signs it out of Jellyfin (for an old install or a TV you no longer use); a TV still in use signs in again with the New devices rights.
