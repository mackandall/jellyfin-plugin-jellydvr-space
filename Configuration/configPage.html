Archive storage

Tried in order: a recording goes to the first location for its kind that keeps its own free-space floor. Each folder must be inside one of your libraries (e.g. a TV Shows or Movies folder), or moved recordings would disappear from Jellyfin. Recordings keep their show / season folders below it.
Separated by commas. Sports recordings go to "Sports" locations first, then to TV or Movies ones. The item menu's "Move to archive…" offers the locations for the recording's kind.

Sort by guide category

Jellyfin files movies and series in their own folders; everything else (comedy specials, one-off games, …) lands in the general recording folder. Rules here move those by their guide categories, tried in order: the first rule a recording matches decides where it goes. Recordings no rule matches stay put. Empty destination = the movie recording folder. Checked with the space check (every few minutes and after each recording).
